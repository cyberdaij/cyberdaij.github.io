Home

    My name is Jun Dai. I'm currently an Associate Professor in Department of Computer Science at Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI). My research interests mainly lie in network and distributed system security, AI and security, with focus on intrusion detection, vulnerability analysis, secure programming, and cybersecurity education. I earned my PhD degree in Information Sciences and Technology with a specialization in cybersecurity from the Pennsylvania State University (PSU) in 2014, under the mentorship of Prof. Peng Liu. Prior to that, I got my BS degree in Information Security in 2007 and my MS degree in Network Control in 2010, both from University of Science and Technology of China (USTC).

    Recruitment: I'm looking for self-motivated students at all levels (undergraduates, master, and PhD). I have PhD positions with full RA/TA support. Please refer to WPI Current Rates for stipend information (e.g., $3,334.5/month suggested for PhD candidates). If you are interested, please email me. Post-doctoral and visiting researchers or students are also welcome.
